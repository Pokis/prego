# Universal guidance

Pregnancy, Clearly has one generic reader edition. The product does not ask for a country, switch appointment packs or expose different national rule sets.

## Writing boundary

- Give the clearest broadly applicable pregnancy baseline the evidence supports.
- State concrete do and don’t guidance where a useful general rule exists.
- Use “ask your doctor or midwife” when the answer depends on medical history, symptoms, medicine, exact product, pregnancy complication or care schedule.
- Present appointment dates as common planning windows, never guaranteed universal bookings.
- Tell readers to use their own emergency number and maternity contact route without building a location database.

## What universal does not mean

Universal presentation does not mean every recommendation is identical for every person. The site is the complete general guide; the reader’s clinician is the source for individual decisions. Do not solve variation by adding a country selector, external reading list or vague “rules vary” filler.

## Localization

Translation changes language, not the care-pack model. A locale edition must preserve the same public structure and doctor-advice boundary unless the owner explicitly changes product direction.
