# Medical safety and escalation

Pregnancy, Clearly is educational. It does not diagnose, triage interactively, approve medicines, choose treatment or replace prenatal, postpartum, pediatric, emergency or mental-health care.

## Three tiers

1. **Common:** normal variation and practical self-care.
2. **Care team:** symptoms or decisions that deserve timely professional discussion.
3. **Urgent:** immediate care or local emergency service.

The tier is content metadata and a visual language. Do not communicate urgency by color alone.

## Prohibited patterns

- “This is definitely normal.”
- “This medicine is safe in pregnancy.”
- “Stop taking…” without individual prescriber involvement.
- A score that labels a pregnancy low or high risk.
- A missed milestone interpreted as a diagnosis.

Evaluation builds show a visible clinical-review-pending banner and emit `noindex,nofollow`. Remove that state only as part of a real clinically approved release; never hide it merely for visual polish.

Reader help uses three stable levels: ordinary information, amber contact-your-care-team guidance, and red urgent or emergency action. Do not collapse amber and red into one generic warning block.

- Reassurance that overrides severe, sudden, worsening or unfamiliar symptoms.

Urgent pages remain reachable from every route. Maternal warning guidance applies during pregnancy and within a year after birth even though the main product timeline ends at six months.
