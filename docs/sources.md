# Sources

Internal sources make authored guidance traceable without turning the reader experience into a citation browser.

Prefer, in order:

1. WHO and other international public-health authorities.
2. National health authorities and statutory guidance.
3. National guidelines.
4. Established professional bodies.

Commercial guides and community stories may reveal reader questions but should not be the sole support for a health claim. Record the canonical URL, supported claim and available publication or update information. Source metadata provides internal editorial provenance.
